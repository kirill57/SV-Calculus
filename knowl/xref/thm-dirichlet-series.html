Theorem 15.6.18 Dirichlet’s test.

Suppose the partial sums \(A_n=\sum_{k=1}^n a_k\) are bounded in absolute value, and \(b_n\ge0\) decreases to zero. Then \(\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_nb_n\) converges.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
If \(|A_n|\le M\text{,}\) with \(A_0=0\text{,}\) then \(|A_{p,k}|=|A_k-A_{p-1}|\le2M\text{.}\) Summation by parts gives
🔗
\begin{equation*} \left|\sum_{k=p}^q a_kb_k\right|\le2M\left(b_q+\sum_{k=p}^{q-1}(b_k-b_{k+1})\right)=2Mb_p. \end{equation*}
🔗
The bound tends to zero independently of \(q\text{.}\) The Cauchy criterion for series proves convergence.
