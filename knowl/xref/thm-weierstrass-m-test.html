Theorem 16.11.1 Weierstrass M-test.

Suppose \(|f_n(x)|\le M_n\) for every \(x\in D\text{,}\) where \(M_n\ge0\) and \(\sum M_n\) converges. Then \(\sum f_n(x)\) converges absolutely at every point and uniformly on \(D\text{.}\) Its remainder satisfies
🔗
\begin{equation*} |F(x)-S_N(x)|\le\sum_{n=N+1}^\infty M_n\qquad(x\in D). \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Absolute convergence at each point follows by numerical comparison. For every \(q\ge p\text{,}\)
🔗
\begin{equation*} \left|\sum_{n=p}^q f_n(x)\right|\le\sum_{n=p}^q M_n. \end{equation*}
🔗
The Cauchy criterion makes the right side arbitrarily small for sufficiently large \(p\text{,}\) independently of \(q\) and \(x\text{.}\) Letting \(q\to\infty\) yields the remainder bound. The tails of the convergent majorant series tend to zero, so this same bound proves uniform convergence.
