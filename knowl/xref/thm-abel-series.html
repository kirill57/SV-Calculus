Theorem 15.6.20 Abel’s test for series.

If \(\sum a_n\) converges and \((b_n)\) is a bounded monotone real sequence, then \(\sum a_nb_n\) converges.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
By monotone convergence, \(b_n\to b\) for some real \(b\text{.}\) Put \(c_n=b_n-b\text{.}\) The sequence \(c_n\) is monotone and tends to zero. The partial sums of \(a_n\) are bounded because they converge. Dirichlet’s test, with a sign reversal if needed, proves convergence of \(\sum a_nc_n\text{.}\) Since \(\sum a_nb_n=b\sum a_n+\sum a_nc_n\) at the level of partial sums, Abel’s test follows.
